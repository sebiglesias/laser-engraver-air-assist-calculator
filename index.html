Frequently Asked Questions

Why is air assist crucial for laser cutting?
Air assist blows a continuous concentrated jet of air through the focal cone directly into the laser kerf. This immediately suppresses combustive flaming, blows away combusted vaporized carbon (eliminating charred edges), and protects the expensive optical focus lens from smoke soot deposits.
Can I use too much air during raster engraving?
Yes. High air velocity during raster engraving blows hot vaporized ash across the cold wood surface, creating dirty smudges. Engraving requires very low airflow (10–15 L/min) simply to protect the lens.
Can I cut PVC or vinyl with a laser?
NEVER cut PVC or polyvinyl chloride vinyl on any laser cutter. The thermal decomposition releases pure chlorine gas, which reacts with humidity to form hydrochloric acid—instantly corroding laser rails and causing lethal pulmonary lung damage.
